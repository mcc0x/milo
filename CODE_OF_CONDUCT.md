# Milo community code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to ask questions, report problems, review code, and contribute. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and direct contact about the project.

- Critique the change, not the person. Explain the problem and leave room for someone to disagree or be new.
- Respect boundaries. No harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual attention, or publication of someone else’s private information.
- If someone asks you to stop contacting them, stop. Do not retaliate against someone for raising a concern.

## When something goes wrong

For abuse on GitHub, you can [report it directly to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse) without posting publicly. To ask Milo maintainers to intervene, contact a maintainer privately if you already have a channel. Otherwise you may [request a private follow-up](https://github.com/mcc0x/milo/issues/new) without naming anyone or describing the incident. You do not have to confront the person involved. If the concern involves a maintainer, you can use the GitHub route instead.

Share only what is needed: a link, the behavior of concern, and any immediate safety or privacy needs. Maintainers should limit sharing to people needed to address the report. Depending on severity and repetition, they may ask for a change, remove content, restrict participation, or block someone from the project.
